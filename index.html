GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I am an Avionics Engineering graduate from Aviation and Aerospace University Bangladesh (AAUB), with a strong academic and research background in antennas, microwave engineering, electromagnetic systems, and radar technologies.

My research journey began with the design and optimization of UWB microstrip patch antennas and MIMO configurations. Through my academic projects and research experience, my interests have gradually expanded toward radar sensing, wireless communications, and Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) for 6G networks.

Currently, I am particularly interested in intelligent and learning-based ISAC systems, adaptive radar signal processing, secure UAV networks, and energy-efficient wireless sensing.
