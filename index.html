About

I am a recent B.Sc. graduate in Avionics Engineering from Aviation and Aerospace University, Bangladesh. My academic and research interests focus on RF and microwave engineering, antenna design, wireless communication, radar signal processing, and intelligent sensing.

My research experience includes antenna design, adaptive signal processing, OTFS-based waveform studies, and radar-oriented sensing concepts. I am interested in combining electromagnetic design, mathematical modelling, simulation, and signal processing to investigate practical communication and sensing problems.
