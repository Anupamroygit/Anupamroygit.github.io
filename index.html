About

I am an Avionics Engineering graduate (BSc, March 2026) from Aviation and Aerospace University, Bangladesh, with a CGPA of 3.52/4.00. I come from Khulna, and my work sits where antennas, radar and wireless communication meet.

My undergraduate thesis was a compact 4-element UWB microstrip MIMO antenna, which I designed, fabricated and measured. I have since published and presented work on patch antennas, OTFS waveforms and 6G integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), and I review manuscripts for the IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society.

From 2022 to 2025 I was a research assistant on the university rocket project, working on the electrical power system.

Research interests

  • Antenna design: UWB, multiband and reconfigurable microstrip patch antennas, MIMO isolation, defected ground structures.
  • Radar signal processing: Pulse-radar simulation and parameter analysis in MATLAB/Simulink.
  • 6G ISAC: Foundation models and agentic learning for radar-native scene understanding.
  • Wireless waveforms: OTFS design and implementation, adaptive filtering.

Undergraduate Thesis: UWB Microstrip Patch Antenna and MIMO

  • Method: Designed and fabricated a compact 4-element UWB MIMO antenna covering 3.1–10.25 GHz, using spike-shaped radiators and defected ground structures to improve isolation and reduce mutual coupling.
